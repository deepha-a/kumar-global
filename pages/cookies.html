1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide reporting information.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Kumar Global) are called "first-party cookies". Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies". Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality (e.g., interactive maps, embedded fonts, and social media buttons).

2 Why We Use Cookies

We use first-party and third-party cookies for several technical and operational reasons:

  • Essential Operation: Some cookies are strictly necessary for security and essential page navigation.
  • User Preference Retention: Storing session choices and active state preferences.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: Understanding visitor volume, server performance, and page loading speeds to optimize website performance.
  • Embedded Services: Enabling interactive Google Maps embedded on our contact page and LinkedIn business links.

5 Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have the right to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies by default, but you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies or alert you when a cookie is placed.

Browser Configuration Instructions

To disable or clear cookies in your specific web browser, follow the links below:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data. (Chrome Help Guide)
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data. (Firefox Help Guide)
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies. (Safari Help Guide)
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions. (Edge Help Guide)

Note: Disabling essential cookies may impact specific website features, such as interactive map loading and quick inquiry forms.
